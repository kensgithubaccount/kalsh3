# Model Governance

Every model and calibrator is versioned, evaluated out of sample, compared against executable market price, and paired with uncertainty and abstention. Frozen forecasts are immutable. Champion/challenger promotion requires calibration, market-relative skill, base/adverse after-cost economics, diverse settled outcomes, and rollback metadata.

Learning can make bounded, reversible changes to approved weights and thresholds. It cannot change credentials, code, settlement semantics, signer policy, source catalogs, or hard financial limits.

## M8 forecast separation

Market references, independent fundamentals, and market-anchored ensembles are separately versioned.
Independent models prohibit Kalshi and external-market features. Calibration is chronological and grouped
by event; small samples use identity or shrinkage. Model cards and artifacts remain development/research/
shadow/challenger only, with production influence NONE. Real settled evidence is currently insufficient.
