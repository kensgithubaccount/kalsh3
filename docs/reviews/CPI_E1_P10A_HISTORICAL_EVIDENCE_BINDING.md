## Binding and rejection policy

The join requires exact event grammar, event-to-market identity, predicate
month, P9A semantic comparator/threshold, initial-release month/value, finalized
settlement result, and P9A evidence/request identities. A candle is eligible
only when `end_period_ts < market_close`. A usable executable quote is a
strictly pre-cutoff YES ask with neither YES entry boundary (`yes_ask != 1.00`
and `yes_bid != 0.00`). No midpoint, last trade, retrospective volume, or
post-cutoff candle is used.

The 112 rejected rows are: 111 `missing authoritative initial-release truth`
rows from the 14 non-overlapping later events, and one
`predicate/reference month mismatch`. The three known historical placeholder
rule strings in October 2021 are admitted only because P9A independently binds
their exact GT threshold and the event month remains separately checked.

## Market baseline

Each sibling is scored against its executable YES ask, averaged within its
event, and then event-averaged; siblings therefore do not create independent
statistical weight. The report generated by `scripts/run_cpi_e1_p10a.py` is:

- Brier score: **0.1354339156**.
- Log loss: **0.4447426883**, with probabilities clipped to `[0.01, 0.99]`
  before natural-log scoring.
- Calibration bins `[0,.2)`, `[.2,.4)`, `[.4,.6)`, `[.6,.8)`, `[.8,1)` have
  row counts 68, 29, 22, 26, 75 and observed rates 0.0588, 0.2069, 0.5455,
  0.7308, 0.8933 respectively.
- Fresh (`age <= 1h`) quote diagnostics: 67 rows / 20 events, Brier
  0.11495820896, log loss 0.36073450518.
- Stale (`age > 1h`) quote diagnostics: 151 rows / 35 events, Brier
  0.12371920530, log loss 0.42237181765.

These are quote-evidence diagnostics, not fill truth. The frozen evidence does
not establish depth, queue position, fill probability, slippage, fees, or
after-cost economics.

## Modelability gate

Admissible existing input: prior P8 initial-release values whose independently
proven `release_instant` is strictly before the relevant cutoff. Missing evidence
is an independent contemporaneous forecast or survey vintage with exact public
availability timestamps, plus point-in-time release-calendar snapshots for every
cutoff. Consequently no challenger model is scored or selected in P10A, and no
revised historical value is manufactured as a feature.

Verdict: **PREDICTOR EVIDENCE ACQUISITION REQUIRED**.

The smallest next profitability-relevant action is to acquire and freeze one
small, independently timestamped contemporaneous predictor family for the same
46-event domain, then preregister a rolling prior-release baseline and one
transparent challenger before opening the final test partition. Fees remain a
separate attachment boundary keyed by actual decision timestamp.

## Not established

P10A does not establish market-relative predictive signal, statistical
significance, causal predictability, executable fills, fee-adjusted edge,
profitability, capacity, sizing, risk authorization, or production readiness.
