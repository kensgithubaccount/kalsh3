# Product Requirements

The platform combines a security-separated intelligence and learning system, deterministic trading system, private web product, and production-grade Kalshi integration. Its objective is realistic long-run after-cost expected value subject to capital survival, calibration, uncertainty, liquidity, reliability, compliance, and auditability.

Abstention is a first-class outcome. No component may claim guaranteed profitability. LLM output never authorizes or sizes an order. Production writes require isolated credentials and explicit human activation and always restart disarmed.
